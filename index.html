Editorial Foundation

Petroleum development, public responsibility, and commercial reality in one frame.

Upstream Atlas is built on the book Exploration and Exploitation of Petroleum Resources in West Africa, then reframed as a web-first intelligence surface. The aim is not only to publish chapters online, but to organise them into a platform that supports serious petroleum, fiscal, and governance analysis.

This approach keeps the intellectual depth of the source material while presenting it in a format that feels credible to policy teams, operators, researchers, and commercial readers.

Core lens
