About the Book

Petroleum development, public responsibility, and fiscal strategy.

This book examines how West African states can understand, regulate, and benefit from petroleum exploration and exploitation. It connects technical petroleum operations with legal, fiscal, institutional, and governance questions that shape outcomes for governments, investors, and citizens.

Core lens

Technical operations are read alongside fiscal design, state capacity, and long-run public accountability.

Who This Book Is For

Designed for readers who work across energy, policy, and research.

Students & Researchers

Comprehensive academic resource covering petroleum value chains, fiscal systems, and governance frameworks.

Policy Makers & Regulators

Comparative analysis of tax regimes, licensing frameworks, and institutional capacity across West Africa.

Energy Professionals

In-depth technical and commercial insights for upstream operations, from exploration to production.

What You Will Learn

A practical map of the region’s petroleum questions.

How the petroleum value chain works

Upstream, midstream, and downstream roles in a regional development strategy.

Where states create leverage

Licensing, exploration oversight, production planning, and abandonment obligations.

How fiscal regimes shape outcomes

Royalties, cost recovery, profit oil, taxation, and state participation.

Why governance determines performance

Political stability, transparency, corruption risk, and institutional capacity.
